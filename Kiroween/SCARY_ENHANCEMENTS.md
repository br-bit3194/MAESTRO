# 🎃 Scary Landing Page Enhancements

## 💀 What Was Added

I've transformed the landing page into a **truly terrifying experience** with intense visual effects and a much darker atmosphere!

### 🔴 New Scary Effects

#### 1. **Screen Glitch** (`ScreenGlitch.tsx`)
- Random screen glitches every 3-8 seconds
- RGB split effect (chromatic aberration)
- Horizontal scan lines
- White flash overlays
- Creates that "haunted monitor" feel

#### 2. **Red Vignette** (`RedVignette.tsx`)
- Pulsing blood-red vignette around screen edges
- Creates claustrophobic, ominous atmosphere
- Continuously pulses to increase tension
- Makes it feel like you're looking through blood-stained glass

#### 3. **Flickering Lights** (`FlickeringLights.tsx`)
- Overhead light that flickers erratically
- Emergency red light pulse in corner
- Simulates power failures and danger
- Adds to the "haunted data center" vibe
